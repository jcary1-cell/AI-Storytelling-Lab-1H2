Welcome & Lab Philosophy

Captions available in video settings Watch on YouTube

In this self-paced module, you will step away from traditional technical exercises and enter the world of narrative foresight. Drawing inspiration from AI and the Art of Being Human by Jeffery Abbot and Andrew Maynard, we believe that understanding the societal impact of AI requires us to story it.

You will pair with generative AI tools as co-authors to write short, fictional narratives, fables, or future scenarios. Your objective isn't a literary masterpiece—it is to unpack complex human intersections with AI, formulate a clear moral, and distill that moral into a practical strategy for your semester-long AI project.

Self-Paced Lab Steps

1

Step 1: Briefing & Perspective Selection

Frame your story around the central seed question: "What is AI?" You can explore technical facets like algorithmic bias, GPU infrastructure, or neural network attention mechanisms using the resources above.

Next, choose ONE disciplinary perspective to guide your storytelling lens:

Blue Lens

The Builders (STEM/Tech)

Focus on data mechanics, systemic optimization, edge cases, and hardware limits.

Green Lens

The Humanists (Arts/Humanities)

Focus on ethics, lived human experience, aesthetics, narrative truth, and culture.

Yellow Lens

The Strategists (Business/Law)

Focus on governance, market scaling, resource trade-offs, and operational viability.

Pink Lens

The Analysts (Social Sciences)

Focus on power dynamics, community behaviors, equity, and institutional structures.

Purple Lens

Wild Card (Interdisciplinary)

Synthesize your personal background or bridge connections across multiple disciplines.

2

Step 2: Co-Creation & "The Rule of Friction"

Work with your chosen AI assistant (Gemini or Copilot). Apply The Rule of Friction: Never accept the first output! Challenge the AI, inject twists, or shift character motives.

Interactive Prompting Workbench Click to copy prompt to clipboard
"Act as a science fiction author. Write a 400-word fable about a community encountering an AI system that predicts resource shortages. Apply a [Insert Your Lens, e.g., Humanist] perspective."
3

Step 3: Extract the Moral & Design Strategy

Pause and reflect on your finished narrative:

  • The Moral: What fundamental lesson does this fable illustrate about human-AI interaction?
  • The Strategy: Translate that moral into a concrete design principle or risk mitigation strategy for your semester project.
4

Step 4: Asynchronous Gallery Walk on Padlet

Post your finalized story/fable directly onto our class Padlet board embedded below.

Peer Interaction Requirement: Read at least two classmates' stories on the board. Leave constructive comments noting how their disciplinary lens influenced their narrative moral or strategy.

5

Step 5: Canvas Process Submission

Paste your shareable Google Doc link (your template with transcript history, lens selection, and reflection) into the Canvas assignment page for credit.
